Questions

Is it really a one-time purchase?

Yes. You pay once on the App Store and get every feature: $4.99 USD during the launch sale until December 7, normally $19.99. No subscription, no in-app purchases, no ads. Outside the US, the App Store shows the price in your own currency.

Do I need an account or internet connection?

No. BeefCake stores everything on your iPhone, and logging works offline. Only the optional demo-video search needs a connection.

What happens to my data if I get a new phone?

Go to Settings › Export file to save a backup, then use Import file on the new phone.

Does it work in kilograms?

Yes. Choose lbs or kg for lifting, and inches or centimetres for body measurements.

Is there an Android version?

BeefCake Trainer is on iPhone first. Android isn't available yet.
